Privacy Policy

Applies to: Every War Mapped Uploader · Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who we are

Every War Mapped Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Every War Mapped (@EveryWarMapped). It is used by a single person, the channel owner, to publish that channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API. When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, the app receives an OAuth token for the following scopes:

  • youtube.upload – to upload the channel's own video files;
  • youtube.force-ssl – to set video metadata (title, description, tags, privacy and scheduled publish time), upload subtitle tracks and custom thumbnails, and read the channel's own name and ID to confirm the correct channel is connected.

The app does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube users, and it does not access any account other than the channel owner's.

How data is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a local configuration file. It is never sent to any server other than Google's. Video files, thumbnails and subtitles are created locally and sent only to YouTube.

Sharing

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access. Deleting the local configuration file also removes the stored token.
