Every War Mapped

Every war in history, on animated maps.

Every War Mapped is a YouTube channel that tells every war in human history on animated maps, in chronological order, from the first recorded war to the conflicts of today. Episodes are researched from historical sources, which are listed in every video description.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@EveryWarMapped

Every War Mapped Uploader

Every War Mapped Uploader is a private, single-user tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own finished videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and subtitle tracks, and schedule their publication. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's YouTube account.
